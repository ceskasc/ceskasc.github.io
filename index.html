04About

A bias for clarity.
A taste for detail.

The best software feels obvious to use and surprisingly uneventful to maintain.

I care about clean boundaries, useful defaults, responsive behavior, keyboard access, loading and empty states, performance, and the dozens of small decisions that create trust.

My preference is simple: fewer fragile abstractions, stronger fundamentals, and enough visual discipline that the product — not the decoration — stays in focus.

01
Make structure legible.

Readable systems are easier to extend, debug, and hand over.

02
Design behavior, not decoration.

States, timing, hierarchy, and feedback are part of the product.

03
Prefer durable over clever.

A strong solution should still make sense after the initial excitement is gone.

TypeScript/React/Next.js/Node.js/APIs/Product UI/Systems thinking
